# Identities Playbook — Practical Actions & Evidence Guide

Each of your six desired identities is built through repeated, concrete actions. Use this playbook when planning your week or looking for the smallest meaningful step to take today.

---

## 1. Entrepreneur / Startup CEO

**Core Belief:** *"I build and validate real products and turn ideas into reality."*

### What Counts as Real Evidence
- Shipped a working feature, prototype, or landing page.
- Talked to a potential user, gathered feedback, or validated a problem hypothesis.
- Defined scope, cut unnecessary complexity, or solved a core technical/product blocker.

### Concrete Examples (From Your System)
- Implemented a feature for my product MVP.
- Drafted a validation experiment or user interview script.
- Deployed a small update or fixed a critical user-facing bug.

### Two-Minute Starter (When Motivation Is Low)
- Open the project workspace and write down one concrete function, screen, or validation question to tackle next.

---

## 2. Freelancer

**Core Belief:** *"I continuously sharpen market-value skills and deliver work that clients pay for."*

### What Counts as Real Evidence
- Practiced or learned a technical/professional skill with clear market demand.
- Searched for suitable freelance opportunities or reached out to potential clients.
- Delivered client work, hit a milestone, or made measurable progress toward getting paid.

### Concrete Examples (From Your System)
- Sent a thoughtful, tailored freelance proposal.
- Built a reusable demo or portfolio piece showcasing a market-value skill.
- Completed and delivered a client task or milestone.

### Two-Minute Starter (When Motivation Is Low)
- Review 1 freelance listing or open your current deliverable and complete 2 minutes of focused setup.

---

## 3. Social Person / Creator

**Core Belief:** *"I initiate meaningful conversations, contribute to communities, and create publicly."*

### What Counts as Real Evidence
- Initiated a conversation with someone instead of waiting for others to reach out first.
- Contributed a thoughtful post, answer, or discussion in an online community (such as Reddit).
- **Weekly Cadence:** Created, shared, scripted, or developed a concrete idea for YouTube content.
- Attended a social event, meetup, or took a step toward meeting new people.

### Concrete Examples (From Your System)
- Outlined a YouTube video concept with title, hook, and 3 key talking points.
- Wrote a helpful, detailed reply in a Reddit community.
- Reached out to a friend or acquaintance to start a conversation or plan a meetup.

### Two-Minute Starter (When Motivation Is Low)
- Send one genuine message to a friend/peer, or jot down one YouTube video idea in your notes.

---

## 4. Avid Reader

**Core Belief:** *"I read out of genuine curiosity and enjoyment as a natural part of my day."*

### What Counts as Real Evidence
- Read a book for enjoyment—not just to rush to the last page or check a box.
- Explored history, technology news, or a deep subject out of authentic curiosity.
- Made reading an easy, frictionless part of the daily routine.

### Concrete Examples (From Your System)
- Read 15 pages because I was genuinely curious about the topic.
- Read an in-depth historical or technical article instead of scrolling short-form feeds.
- Kept a book next to my desk/bed and read before sleep.

### Two-Minute Starter (When Motivation Is Low)
- Pick up your current book (or a curiosity topic) and read just 2 pages with zero pressure to continue if you don't want to.

---

## 5. Disciplined Person

**Core Belief:** *"I know my top commitments, start when planned, act without waiting for motivation, and always return after a miss."*

### What Counts as Real Evidence
- Identified your most important commitments *before* starting the day.
- Followed through on a commitment even when motivation was low.
- Started an important task at the exact time planned.
- **Never Miss Twice:** Returned to a habit after missing a day instead of abandoning the system.

### Concrete Examples (From Your System)
- Started studying or building at the planned time.
- Wrote down top 2 daily commitments before opening messages or feeds.
- Resumed a habit today after missing it yesterday.

### Two-Minute Starter (When Motivation Is Low)
- Write down your #1 commitment for today on a sticky note or in the dashboard, and work on it for 120 seconds.

---

## 6. Balanced Person

**Core Belief:** *"I enjoy games, stories, and rest without guilt, while protecting my sleep and core priorities."*

### What Counts as Real Evidence
- Enjoyed gaming, movies, series, anime, or leisure *after* honoring important commitments—without guilt.
- Stopped or took a break when entertainment started encroaching on priorities.
- Allowed yourself genuine rest without feeling that every waking hour must be "productive."
- Protected sleep and basic physical needs instead of sacrificing them for either work or late-night screens.

### Concrete Examples (From Your System)
- Played a game guilt-free after completing my daily commitments.
- Closed a game or series at my planned bedtime to protect 8 hours of sleep.
- Took a deliberate rest afternoon without doomscrolling or self-criticism.

### Two-Minute Starter (When Motivation Is Low)
- Decide in advance when your leisure block starts and when you will wrap up for sleep.
